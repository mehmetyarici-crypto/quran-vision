### STEP 00-5 — LINGUISTIC MATRIX & SEMANTIC ANALYSIS (MANDATORY H6)

- **4-Dimensional Linguistic Matrix & Semantic Analysis (Mandatory H6 Protocol):**
  1. **`وَلَا تَقْفُ` (*Wa-la taqfu* — Root `q-f-w`):** Analyzed by Ibn Faris (*Maqayis*, 5/11) and Ibn Manzur (*Lisan al-Arab*, 15/192) from `qafa` (the nape of the neck): *qafawtu atharahu* means walking blindly right behind someone's neck/footsteps or making an unverified accusation (`qadhf`). The prohibition `la taqfu` forbids following, repeating, sharing, or testifying to anything without verified knowledge.
  2. **`مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلْمٌ` (*Ma laysa laka bihi 'ilm* — Epistemic Boundary):** In classical *Usul* and *Kalam* (Al-Zamakhshari, *al-Kashshaf*, 2/664; Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, *Mafatih*, 20/205), `'ilm` denotes justified, verified knowledge grounded in sensory evidence, reliable transmission, or rational proof—excluding conjecture (`zann`), rumor (`isha'ah`), and prejudice (`hawa`).
  3. **`ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْبَصَرَ وَٱلْفُؤَادَ` (*Al-Sam'a wa'l-Basara wa'l-Fu'ad* — Triadic Cognitive Faculties):** Hearing (`al-sam'` — auditory/transmitted data input), Sight (`al-basar` — empirical observation), and the Heart-Mind (`al-fu'ad` — from `fa'ada`, glowing/throbbing, denoting critical reflection and moral judgment).
  4. **`كُلُّ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـُٔولًۭا` (*Kullu ula'ika kana 'anhu mas'ula*):** Every single one of those cognitive faculties will be questioned and held legally and morally accountable (`mas'ul`).

- **Primary Quranic Text (Surah Al-Isra 17:36 — Resm-i Uthmani):**
  > وَلَا تَقْفُ مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلْمٌ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْبَصَرَ وَٱلْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـُٔولًا ﴿٣٦﴾

- **Canonical English Translation (Sahih International):**
  > *"And do not pursue (wa lā taqfu) that of which you have no verified knowledge (‘ilm). Indeed, the hearing (as-sam‘), the sight (al-baṣar), and the heart/intellect (al-fu’ād) — about all those [faculties], one will be questioned and held accountable (mas’ūlā). (36)"*

- **Word-by-Word Lexical & Morphological Breakdown (*Al-Tahlil al-Lafzi*):**
  - `وَلَا تَقْفُ` (Wa lā taqfu): And do not follow, track, assert, or act upon! (Prohibitive jussive from *qafā - yaqfū*, literally 'to follow in someone's footsteps', forbidding unverified assertions, rumors, and baseless judgments).
  - `مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلْمٌ` (mā laysa laka bihī ‘ilm): That regarding which you possess no verified, evidence-backed knowledge (`‘ilm` in the indefinite negation encompasses every form of conjecture, fake news, and false testimony).
  - `إِنَّ ٱلسَّمْعَ` (inna's-sam‘a): Indeed, the faculty of hearing (the auditory channel of transmitted reports and testimony).
  - `وَٱلْبَصَرَ` (wa'l-baṣara): And the faculty of sight (the empirical channel of direct observation and visual evidence).
  - `وَٱلْفُؤَادَ` (wa'l-fu’āda): And the cognitive-moral heart / intellect (`fu’ād` is the seat of critical reasoning, verification, and ethical judgment).
  - `كُلُّ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ` (kullu ulā’ika): Every single one of those faculties (`ulā’ika`, normally used for rational persons, personifies the cognitive faculties as legal witnesses).
  - `كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـُٔولًا` (kāna ‘anhu mas’ūlā): Shall be interrogated and held legally and morally accountable.

- **Classical Exegetical Synthesis on Lexical Order (Fakhr al-Din al-Razi & Ibn 'Ashur):**
  - `[CITATION]`: Fakhr al-Din al-Razi (*Mafatih al-Ghayb*, Vol. 20, pp. 209–212) and Ibn 'Ashur (*al-Tahrir wa'l-Tanwir*, Vol. 15, p. 99) explain that `lā taqfu` establishes a universal epistemic prohibition against unverified claims across theology, judicial testimony, and public discourse, while `sam‘ -> baṣar -> fu’ād` maps the exact cognitive pipeline from sensory input to critical verification.
  - `[ANALYSIS]`: In the age of algorithmic disinformation and deepfakes, Al-Isra 17:36 elevates epistemic verification (`tabayyun`) from a technical skill to a sacred moral trust (`mas’ūliyyah`).
- **Historical & Contextual Synthesis (*Siyaq-Sibaq*):**
  - `[ANALYSIS]`: Within the broader canonical sequence of **Surah Al-Isra' (17:36)**, every lexical unit above operates as part of an integrated legal, moral, and sociological system. Classical philologists and *Usul al-Fiqh* authorities emphasize that isolating individual words from their morphological form, syntactic conditions, and intra-surah context (*siyaq-sibaq*) distorts the normative intent of revelation; therefore, the word-by-word breakdown above serves as the binding philological anchor for all subsequent systemic, empirical, and policy steps in this monograph.

- **Logical Condition-Consequence Structure:**
  - *Universal Epistemic Prohibition (`Wa-la taqfu ma laysa laka bihi 'ilm`):* Never pursue, assert, judge, or amplify any claim lacking verified knowledge (`'ilm`).
  - *Ontological & Moral Justification (`inna's-sam'a wa'l-basara wa'l-fu'ada kullu ula'ika kana 'anhu mas'ula`):* Because human cognitive faculties (hearing, sight, and critical reason) are divine trusts subject to individual accountability.
