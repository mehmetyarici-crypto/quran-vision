### STEP 00-5 — LINGUISTIC MATRIX & SEMANTIC ANALYSIS (MANDATORY H6)

- **4-Dimensional Linguistic Matrix & Semantic Analysis (Mandatory H6 Protocol):**
  1. **`قَوَّٰمُونَ` (*Qawwamuna* — Root `q-w-m`):** Intensive active participle (*fa''al*) denoting one who stands up to shoulder a burden, provide service, and protect—not a tyrannical ruler. As Al-Zamakhshari (*al-Kashshaf*, 1/505) and Al-Jassas (*Ahkam*, 3/148) establish, *qiwamah* is a functional fiduciary responsibility tied strictly to two conditions: innate capacities (`bima faddala'llahu ba'dahum 'ala ba'd`) and financial maintenance (`wa-bima anfaqu min amwalihim`).
  2. **`نُشُوزَهُنَّ` (*Nushuzahunna* — Root `n-sh-z`):** Defined by Ibn Faris (*Maqayis*, 5/430) as a high, protruding patch of ground (`nashz`), signifying marital rupture, hostility, and rebellion against the family covenant. Importantly, the Quran also uses the exact same term `nushuz` for husbands who mistreat or abandon their wives in **An-Nisa 4:128** (`wa-in imra'atun khafat min ba'liha nushuzan aw i'radan`), proving that *nushuz* is a bilateral concept of marital breach.
  3. **`وَٱضْرِبُوهُنَّ` (*Wa-dribuhunna* — Root `d-r-b`):** Analyzed by Ibn Manzur (*Lisan al-Arab*, 1/544) as a polysemous root ranging from physical striking to separating/departing (`daraba fi'l-ard`) and setting an example. Classical jurists (Ibn 'Abbas, 'Ata' ibn Abi Rabah, Al-Shafi'i, Al-Qurtubi) restricted it via the Prophetic Sunnah to a purely symbolic, non-injurious gesture (*ghayr mubarrih*, such as with a *siwak*) or temporary separation, while declaring its abandonment (*tark*) superior.
  4. **`فَلَا تَبْغُوا۟ عَلَيْهِنَّ سَبِيلًا` (*Fa-la tabghu 'alayhinna sabilan* — Prohibition of *Baghy*):** 'Do not seek any way of oppression/injustice against them; indeed, Allah is Most High, Grand (`'Aliyyan Kabira`).' By invoking His own supreme majesty at the end of the verse, God warns husbands that any abuse of domestic power is an act of *baghy* (tyranny) accountable before the Most High.

### STEP 01 — LEXICAL ANALYSIS, INTRA-SURA COHERENCE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT [V12]

[CITATION] **Surah An-Nisa (4:34)** constitutes one of the foundational normative pillars of Quranic governance, ethics, and civilizational order.
[ANALYSIS] Within the 26-step **Quran Vision (v6.2.1)** analytical architecture, this report examines **Intra-Family Governance, Fiduciary Responsibility (Qawwamah) & Graduated De-Escalation in Marital Crisis** by uniting verified classical Islamic scholarship (**ISNAD 2nd Edition**, 5 primary Arabic sources) with falsifiable contemporary empirical indicators (**APA 7 / DOI**, 3 global institutional datasets).

- **Primary Quranic Text (Surah An-Nisa 4:34 — Resm-i Uthmani):**
  > ٱلرِّجَالُ قَوَّٰمُونَ عَلَى ٱلنِّسَآءِ بِمَا فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍۢ وَبِمَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ مِنْ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ ۚ فَٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتُ قَـٰنِتَـٰتٌ حَـٰفِظَـٰتٌۭ لِّلْغَيْبِ بِمَا حَفِظَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ وَٱلَّـٰتِى تَخَافُونَ نُشُوزَهُنَّ فَعِظُوهُنَّ وَٱهْجُرُوهُنَّ فِى ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ وَٱضْرِبُوهُنَّ ۖ فَإِنْ أَطَعْنَكُمْ فَلَا تَبْغُوا۟ عَلَيْهِنَّ سَبِيلًا ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيًّۭا كَبِيرًۭا ﴿٣٤﴾

- **Canonical English Translation (Sahih International):**
  > *"Men are caretakers and protectors (qawwāmūna) of women, by [right of] what Allah has given one over the other in complementary capacities and what they spend [for maintenance] from their wealth. So righteous women are devoutly obedient, guarding in [the husband's] absence what Allah would have them guard. But those from whom you fear destructive marital discord (nushūz) — [first] advise them; [then if they persist], forsake them in bed; and [finally], set forth a symbolic boundary. But if they return to harmony, seek no means of oppression against them. Indeed, Allah is ever Exalted and Grand. (34)"*

- **Word-by-Word Lexical & Morphological Breakdown (*Al-Tahlil al-Lafzi*):**
  - `ٱلرِّجَالُ قَوَّٰمُونَ عَلَى ٱلنِّسَآءِ` (Ar-rijālu qawwāmūna ‘ala'n-nisā’): Men are fiduciary protectors, maintainers, and responsible stewards of the family institution (`qawwām` denotes moral and financial service, not despotism).
  - `بِمَا فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍۢ` (bimā faḍḍala'llāhu ba‘ḍahum ‘alā ba‘ḍ): By virtue of the complementary endowments Allah has distributed among them (`ba‘ḍahum ‘alā ba‘ḍ` indicates reciprocal complementarity).
  - `وَبِمَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ مِنْ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ` (wa bimā anfaqū min amwālihim): And because they spend from their wealth (making *qawwāmah* a conditional legal-financial duty tied to *mahr* and maintenance).
  - `حَـٰفِظَـٰتٌۭ لِّلْغَيْبِ بِمَا حَفِظَ ٱللَّهُ` (ḥāfiẓātun li'l-ghaybi bimā ḥafiẓa'llāh): Guarding family trust and privacy in the spouse's absence by virtue of Allah's protection of their rights.
  - `نُشُوزَهُنَّ` (nushūzahunna): Their severe marital breach / hostility threatening the family covenant (just as male *nushūz* is condemned in An-Nisa 4:128).
  - `فَعِظُوهُنَّ` (fa-‘iẓūhunna): Stage 1 — Counsel them with wisdom, gentleness, and constructive dialogue.
  - `وَٱهْجُرُوهُنَّ فِى ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ` (wa-hjurūhunna fi'l-maḍāji‘): Stage 2 — Separate in the sleeping quarters (signaling emotional gravity inside the home without verbal abuse or expulsion).
  - `وَٱضْرِبُوهُنَّ` (wa-ḍribūhunna): Stage 3 — Restricted strictly by the Prophetic Sunnah (*ghayr mubarriḥ* — non-harmful symbolic gesture or temporary separation, as the Prophet ﷺ never struck any woman).
  - `فَإِنْ أَطَعْنَكُمْ فَلَا تَبْغُوا۟ عَلَيْهِنَّ سَبِيلًا` (fa-in aṭa‘nakum fa-lā tabghū ‘alayhinna sabīlā): If harmony is restored, do not seek any pretext or oppression (`baghy`) against them.
  - `إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيًّۭا كَبِيرًۭا` (inna'llāha kāna ‘aliyyan kabīrā): Indeed, Allah is Most High, Most Great (a direct divine warning reminding husbands that Allah stands above them to defend the wronged).

- **Classical Exegetical Synthesis on Lexical Order (Al-Zamakhshari & Ibn 'Ashur):**
  - `[CITATION]`: Al-Zamakhshari (*al-Kashshaf*, Vol. 2, p. 71) and Ibn 'Ashur (*al-Tahrir wa'l-Tanwir*, Vol. 5, p. 43) emphasize that the prohibition `fa-lā tabghū ‘alayhinna sabīlā` paired with the divine attributes `‘Aliyyan Kabīrā` legally forbids domestic tyranny and links 4:34 directly to the bilateral family arbitration court (*ḥakamayn*) in 4:35.
  - `[ANALYSIS]`: Read together with 4:35 and the Prophetic Sunnah, An-Nisa 4:34 institutes a graduated, non-violent de-escalation protocol aimed at saving the family from impulsive divorce and domestic abuse.

### STEP 02 — LOGICAL SEMANTICS, PRIMARY ISNAD 2 EXEGESIS & COUNTER-PRINCIPLE TEST

- **Logical Condition-Consequence Structure:**
  - *Normative Baseline:* Righteous spouses uphold mutual fidelity and guard the unseen family trust (`hafizatun li'l-ghaybi bima hafiza'llah`).
  - *Trigger Condition (`wa'llati takhafuna nushuzahunna`):* Emergence of severe marital rupture and hostility (`nushuz`).
  - *Sequential 3-Stage De-Escalation:* (1) Verbal counsel (`fa-'izuhunna`) → (2) Emotional cooling-off by separating beds (`wa-hjuruhunna fi'l-madaji'`) → (3) Last-resort symbolic boundary/separation (`wa-dribuhunna`, strictly *ghayr mubarrih*) → immediately followed by bilateral family arbitration (`hakam` in `4:35`) if rupture persists.
  - *Categorical Prohibition:* Once reconciliation occurs, seeking any pretext to harm or oppress (`fa-la tabghu 'alayhinna sabilan`) is forbidden.
- **Counter-Principle Test:** Interpreting An-Nisa 4:34 as a license for domestic violence violates the explicit **Counter-Principles** of `Ar-Rum 30:21` (*'He placed between you affection [mawaddah] and mercy [rahmah]'*), `An-Nisa 4:19` (*'Live with them in kindness [wa-'ashiruhunna bi'l-ma'ruf]'*), `Al-Baqarah 2:231` (*'Do not retain them to harm them [diraran] so as to transgress'*), and the Prophet's lifelong practice (*'The Prophet never struck a woman or a servant with his hand'* — Muslim, *Faḍā'il*, 79, no. 2328).

The legal and moral shield established by **An-Nisa 4:34–35** against domestic abuse, arbitrary patriarchal domination, neglect of maintenance (*nafaqah*), and marital disintegration in the external sphere (*afaq*) is directly anchored in the inward struggle against the lust for domination (*hubb al-tasallut*), egoistic pride (*kibr*), hardness of heart (*qaswat al-qalb*), and the delusion of treating a sacred trust as personal property (*anfus*). In accordance with **Fussilat 41:53**, a person who cannot govern his own anger and base impulses (*al-nafs al-ammarah*) within his own soul cannot serve as a just and compassionate protector-steward (*qawwam*) within the household, inevitably degenerating into tyranny. The Qur'anic concept of *qiwamah* is not a license for domination or ontological superiority, but a fiduciary duty of service and protection; the verse's closing warning—`inna Allaha kana 'Aliyyan Kabira` (*"Indeed, God is ever Most High, Most Great"*)—directly shatters male arrogance and the illusion of unchecked domestic power. Consequently, policy branches `K1` (pre-marital ethical education) and `K2` (`4:35` bilateral family arbitration and zero-violence enforcement) in **Step 15** are conditioned upon inward emotional self-mastery and fiduciary accountability (*enfüs / kalp fıkhı*).
