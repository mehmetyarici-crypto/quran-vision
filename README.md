## 🇬🇧 OVERVIEW (ENGLISH)

The **Quran Vision Corpus (`v6.3.0`)** is an open-access, bilingual (**Turkish & English**) academic research platform and strategic foresight engine (**38 Corpus A4 PDF + 3 Congress PDF = 41 PDF** publications). It bridges **classical Islamic hermeneutics (*riwayah*, *dirayah*, *ahkam*, *maqasid*, and *irfani/ishari* exegesis across *Elsine-i Selâse*)** with **contemporary social-scientific empirical indicators (*World Bank, UNDP, WJP, UNESCO, SIPRI, OECD, WHO*)** and **5–10–30 year quantitative Bayesian scenario simulations (`H1–H4`)**.

### ✨ Key Methodological Pillars (`v6.3.0`)
1. **Strict Epistemic Separation (`[CITATION / NAKİL]` vs. `[ANALYSIS / YORUM]`):** Every report rigorously distinguishes immutable scriptural text and classical exegetical transmission from contemporary human policy modeling (*ijtihad*).
2. **371/371 Multi-Server Verified Sources (`241 Shamela + 18 Altafsir + 17 Ganjoor/Persian + 95 Institutional/Academic DOI & TDV` — V16.2 Single Source of Truth):** Verified in `data/sources_registry.yaml` with Hijri/Gregorian death dates, critical edition editors (*tahqiq*), exact volume/page numbers, cumulative Shamela `page_id` links (`shamela.ws/book/<book_id>/<page_id>`), Altafsir Sufi/Ishari links (`tMadhNo=3&tTafsirNo=31`), Ganjoor Persian couplet links (Protocol 1b), SHA-256 content snapshots, `L-2508` duplicate guard, and a `23.2% (86/371 >= 10%)` manual sample audit.
3. **Unified 3-Part Step 13 Irfani / Sufi Hermeneutics (`13.1`, `13.2`, `13.3` — Protocol 1a & 1b):** Every monograph integrates outward institutional order (*afaq*) with inward moral-spiritual purification (*enfüs / fiqh al-batin*) grounded in verbatim classical Arabic and Persian citations from *Ibn 'Arabi (al-Futuhat al-Makkiyya), Al-Qushayri (Lata'if al-Isharat), Al-Ghazali (Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din), Al-Harith al-Muhasibi (al-Ri'ayah li-Huquq Allah), Sahl al-Tustari, Ibn 'Ajibah (al-Bahr al-Madid), and Rumi (Mathnawi-yi Ma'nawi)*.
4. **95 Empirical Institutional & Peer-Reviewed Academic Citations (Protocol 2a & 2b — APA 7 & DOI):** Every strategic claim is paired with falsifiable early-warning thresholds (`th-01` to `th-03`) and obeys **Hume's Guillotine** (explicitly separating empirical *is* from normative *ought*).
5. **Macro-Bayesian Synthesis — Corpus Arithmetic Mean ($\mu$ across 17 reports):** Across all 17 foundational monographs (`QV-2026-001`..`017`), conditional Bayesian scenario updates (`Step 14`) contingent upon `K1–K3` institutional reforms (*Amanah, 'Adl, Shura, Islah, Tabyin, Mizan, Ihsan, Ta'aruf, Ihya', Radm, Fakku Raqabah*) model the 10–30 year probability of societal stability from a **Corpus Arithmetic Mean ($\mu$ across 17 reports)** prior of **$P(S_1) = 30\%$** (`0.301`) to a mean posterior of **$P(S_1|E) = 73\%$** (`0.732`, **+43 percentage points mean shift $\mu$**, and up to **+53 to +56 percentage points** in individual civilizational monographs `QV-2026-011`..`016`). *Methodological Note (Principles 3 & 5): These shifts represent conditional Bayesian scenario updates $P(S_1|E)$, not deterministic causal claims; Popperian falsification thresholds (`th-01..th-03`) are defined in Steps 12, 14, and Z-4.*
6. **40-Minute Competency & Virtue-Based Seminar / Lesson Plans (TYMM) & 41 A4 PDFs:** Each report includes a 4-part (`20.1..20.4`) classroom debate and case-analysis lesson plan and a unified Dual-Table `Z-5` Academic Governance & 100-Point Peer-Review Rubric, compiled into **38 Corpus A4 PDF + 3 Congress A4 PDF = 41 A4 PDF** documents.
